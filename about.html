About Sarga.co

SARGA.CO is a leading 360 sports and entertainment ecosystem company in Indonesia, providing an integrated platform across intellectual property, media rights, venue operation, and live event experiences. Established in 2023 under PT Kuda Pacu Indonesia (PT KPI), SARGA.CO was founded with a clear ambition: to transform the way sports & entertainment is created, experienced, and commercialized in Indonesia.


Our dream is to build a sports ecosystem in Indonesia that can stand alongside the world's leading sports properties, not only as a source of entertainment but as a valuable and sustainable business and a powerful economic force.
- Aryo Djojohadikusumo

Timeline of Sarga.co

  • 2023 - Founded SARGA.CO in Jakarta, focusing on horse sports, including horse archery, equestrian, and horse racing.
  • 2024 - Focused on horse racing and held four horse racing championships across Indonesia.
  • 2025Feb 4th - Launched Indonesia's Horse Racing (IHR).
  • 2025Apr 23rd - Signed an agreement with PT Pulo Mas Jaya to transform JIEPP into an integrated, world-class sports and entertainment venue for racing, equestrian, polo, and horse archery.
  • 2026Mar 26th - Signed a five-year agreement with the Federation Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA) to become the promoter of the FIA Rallycross World Cup Indonesia.
  • 2026Apr 26th - Launched the Indonesia Junior Talent Cup (IJTC).
